Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data.
  • How do I manage or cancel my Coilover Pro subscription? Go to Settings → Manage subscription, or manage it directly through your device's App Store subscription settings.
  • I forgot my password. On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the emailed link to set a new one.
  • Something isn't working right. Email us at the address above with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot — we read every message.
